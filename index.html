Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries.

Everything you need to know about Aura hardware, ecosystem compatibility, and global support.

What makes photonic silicon fundamentally different from traditional chips? +

Traditional microprocessors transmit information via electrons moving through copper wires, which encounter electrical resistance and produce thermal energy (heat). Aura's Photonic M5 uses coherent laser light pulses guided through microscopic crystalline wave-guides. This enables near-infinite bandwidth with virtually zero heat, allowing continuous peak performance without throttling.

Is Aura One Pro compatible with existing USB-C, Wi-Fi, and 5G/6G standards? +

Yes. Aura One Pro features an ultra-high-speed Thunderbolt 5 / USB-C port supporting 80 Gbps transfers, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, and backwards compatibility with all standard cellular GSM/LTE/5G networks alongside its next-generation Quantum 6G antenna.
